Zema Planner

Your wedding, planned in one private place

Every Zema couple gets their own private planner. It’s where we plan your day together, from the timeline and family photos to your invitations and your final films.

Check your dateSee the collections
Your private link

One link for the two of you

No app to download and no password to remember. Open your planner on your phone or laptop and share it with your partner. Your overview shows your collection, your dates and what’s next.

Timeline & locations

The order of your day, in one place

Add each moment with its time, place and notes: getting ready, church, portraits, the reception entrance. Already have a plan as a PDF? Upload it and the planner reads the times and places for you. Your crew sees the parts that matter to them.

Family photo list

Every family photo, in order

List the family and friends groups you want photographed, in the order you want them. On the day we tick each group off, and your family can follow along on their own link to see who is next.

Must-have moments

Nothing important is missed

Tell us the moments that matter most to you: a blessing, a surprise, a special guest. They come with us on the day, together with your wedding party and your notes.

Digital invitations & RSVP

Invitations your guests can answer

Design your invitation from our collections for weddings, christenings, birthdays and graduations, with your own photos, colours and music. Guests reply without logging in, and you see every answer in your dashboard. Included with Platinum, or available as an extra.

Agreement & payments

Clear from the start

Read and sign your agreement online. Your planner shows what you’ve paid, what’s left and when the next payment is due, so there are no surprises.

Delivery

Your photographs and films, privately

Highlight photographs arrive within one to two weeks. Then you choose your favourites from your private gallery, and your films are delivered privately, ready to watch and share with family.

Our crew
