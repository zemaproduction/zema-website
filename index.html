My story

Hi, I’m Abenezer Mesfin.

My journey began with a love for art, photography and the stories that images can tell. With a background in Fine Art and filmmaking, I’ve always been drawn to creating work that feels thoughtful, timeless and meaningful.

With over seven years of experience photographing and filming weddings and events, I founded Zema Production with a vision to bring a fresh, cinematic approach to every celebration, while staying true to the cultures and traditions behind them.

Today, that vision continues to shape everything I create.
