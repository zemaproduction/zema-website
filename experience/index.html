The experience

From first message to final film

Five stages, one team, and everything for your celebration kept in one private place.

I · EnquiryII · Choosing your packageIII · Planning togetherIV · Wedding-day coverageV · Editing
I
Start with your date

Enquiry & availability

  1. Check your date

    Send your date and a few details through the enquiry form or on WhatsApp. We confirm whether we’re available.

  2. Talk it through

    Tell us about your celebration: the church, the reception, any extra days, and what matters most to you.

II
Secure your date

Choosing your package & booking

  1. Build your package

    Choose your package in the online builder, add extras and extra days, and see your selection and total as you go.

  2. Book

    Send your booking, sign your agreement online and pay the booking fee. The rest is paid in stages, with each due date in your planner.

III
Your private Zema Planner

Planning together

  1. Your order of the day

    Your timeline with times and locations for every part of your celebration, or upload the plan you already have.

  2. Family photos & must-haves

    Your family photo groups in order, your wedding party and the moments you don’t want missed.

  3. Invitations & extra days

    Digital invitations and RSVPs when included, and any extra days such as church, Melse or Hammawiti, planned in the same place.

IV
Production, quietly handled

Wedding-day coverage

  1. A briefed crew

    Each crew member receives the timeline, locations and key people for the days they cover, before the day.

  2. Photography & film together

    Photographers and filmmakers work as one crew, with equipment checked before and after every event.

  3. Family photos, in order

    We work through your family photo list group by group, and your family can follow along on their own link.

V
Your story, privately

Editing & final delivery

  1. Highlights first

    Selected highlight photographs within one to two weeks of your wedding.

  2. Your full gallery

    Your remaining photographs in a private online gallery, where you choose your album favourites when your package includes an album.

  3. Your films

    Your cinematic films, edited and delivered privately, ready to watch and share with family.

Ready when you are

Send us your date and we’ll take it from there.
