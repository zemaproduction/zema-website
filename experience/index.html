Experience

From first message to final film

A calm, organised journey, with everything for your day kept in one private place.

01

Enquire & check your date

Send us your date and a few details through the form or on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability and talk through what you have in mind.

02

Choose your collection & book

Pick your collection and extras online, then secure your date with the booking fee. Your agreement is signed online, so everything is clear from the start.

03

Plan together in your private planner

You get your own private planner link to share with us: your timeline, locations, family photo list, must-have moments and notes. If your package includes it, this is also where you design your digital invitations and follow your RSVPs.

04

Your day, beautifully covered

Our crew arrives with your plan in hand and knows who is who. You stay present with your guests, and we take care of the rest across every day of your celebration.

05

Delivery

Highlight photographs within one to two weeks, then your full private gallery to choose your favourites from. Your films are delivered privately, ready to watch and share with your family.

Ready when you are
